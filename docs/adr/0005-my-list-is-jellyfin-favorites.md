# Store My List as Jellyfin Favorites

My List needs somewhere to live. It uses each User's Jellyfin Favorites rather than a list of our own saved on the TV, so it follows the person to every device and needs no storage or syncing from Matinee. In exchange, "save for later" and "favorite" become the same thing.

## Consequences

- A movie or show favorited in another Jellyfin app appears in My List, and removing it from My List removes the Favorite everywhere.
- Saving from an episode saves its show, because My List holds only movies and shows.
- Moving My List to separate storage later would mean guessing which existing Favorites were meant as saved for later.
